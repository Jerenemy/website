Track and return in seconds.

  1. 01

    Scan the location

    At a dining hall or return station, scan the location QR code in the eQoScan app.

  2. 02

    Scan the container

    Scan the unique QR code on the reusable food container to link it to your account.

  3. 03

    Credits update

    Your digital credits update instantly. Return containers to earn credits back for your next meal.
