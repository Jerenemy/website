Large language models are generalists that know a little about everywhere. MoinLLM asks whether one can be made into a local instead: a “Hamburg AI”.

The goal was a model that knows Hamburg, Germany the way someone who lives there does: its culture, history and geography as well as the plain facts.

{% endcall %} {% call ui.sect("02", "The process") %}

Training ran on cluster computing environments and Google’s infrastructure, using instruction-formatted data that taught the model to take on a regional persona and favor local information.

LLM fine-tuning · Cluster computing · Natural language processing · Data formatting

{% endcall %} {% call ui.sect("03", "Results") %}

The training metrics improved steadily, and mean token accuracy passed 61%.

The model also took on the identity it was trained for. When prompted, it described itself as an AI specialized in the history and development of Hamburg.

Asked for directions from the Speicherstadt to the Reeperbahn, the model suggested driving along the Elbe to see the historic port before arriving at the Reeperbahn.
