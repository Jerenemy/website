Sonar for podcasters

Show listeners what they think of your show

Sonar is where listeners rate, review and remember the episodes they love. Put your show's live rating on your site, in your show notes and in your newsletter, and every tap sends a listener to rate you.

{{ find_form() }}

How it works

  1. Find your show above. No sign-up, no account: every show on Podcast Index has a page.
  2. Copy a badge for the show or for a single episode, as HTML, Markdown or a plain link.
  3. Paste it anywhere. The badge updates itself as ratings come in, and tapping it opens your show in Sonar.
