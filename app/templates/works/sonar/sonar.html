{#- ------------------------------------------------------------- the problem -#}

Podcasts are made to be talked about.
Most apps leave you listening alone.

On Sonar you see which episodes your friends picked and what they thought of them, and you can reply.

{#- ------------------------------------------------------------ the features -#}

Friends

See what friends are hearing.

Follow people whose taste you trust, and every episode they rate or review shows up in your feed.

  • A Friends feed of everything they’ve rated and loved
  • Episodes picked for you, from the shows you follow
  • What you listened to recently, one tap away
{{ phone("home", "Sonar's home: episodes for you, recently listened to, and what friends rated.") }}

Reviews

Say what you thought. Reply to friends.

Write a line or a page about any episode. Friends can love your review and reply to it, and you can answer theirs.

{{ phone("review", "A review in Sonar: a rating in dots, the episode it is about, and replies underneath.") }}

Rate

Rate every episode.

Ratings go on five dots in half steps. Tap or slide to rate, mark an episode listened, love the ones that stay with you, and save the rest for later.

Try it. How was the last episode you heard?
