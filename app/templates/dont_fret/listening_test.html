Some of these clips are studio recordings of a real guitarist. The others come from Don't Fret, my Mac app that teaches guitar, which builds its sound from a model of a real guitar. Play each clip, then move its slider to how much it sounds like a recording of a real acoustic guitar.

Time
About ten minutes: {{ seconds }} seconds a clip, as many listens as you like.
Listen on
Headphones, somewhere quiet, at a comfortable volume.
Switch freely
The clips play together, so switching between them keeps your place in the music.

How the test works.

Every piece is the same {{ seconds }} seconds played several ways, matched in loudness and put in a different order for each listener, so only the sound tells them apart. The test follows MUSHRA, the method in ITU-R BS.1534 that audio researchers use to compare sound quality.

Your ratings are stored on this site with your guitar experience and what you listened on, under a random number. I don't keep your name or email, and your network address is kept only as a one-way hash that limits how many tests one network can start in a day.

The studio recordings come from GuitarSet (Xi, Bittner, Pauwels, Ye and Bello, ISMIR 2018), shared under CC BY 4.0.
