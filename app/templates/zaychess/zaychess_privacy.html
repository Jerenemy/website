Privacy Policy

Zaychess {{ zaychess_current_version }} does not collect analytics or tracking data.

Summary

  • This policy applies to Zaychess version {{ zaychess_current_version }}.
  • No account required.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • Game data stays on your device.

Data you provide

If you contact support, you may provide your name, email, and message through the support form. This information is used only to respond to your request and is not sold. Messages are delivered to the developer via the site email backend.

Network use

Multiplayer uses the relay matchmaking server to connect players. There are no gameplay analytics and no account-based telemetry.

Local storage

Zaychess stores saved games (.chesslog), preferences, and local runtime data on your machine. There is no required cloud account or cloud sync.

Changes

This policy currently corresponds to Zaychess {{ zaychess_current_version }}. If the policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions? Use the support form at jeremyzay.com/zaychess/support.
