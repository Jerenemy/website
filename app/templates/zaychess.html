Zaychess

A Chess App Built to Feel Good to Play.
Local hot-seat, deeply configurable AI, and a tactile, premium design.

🍎 Get it on the Mac App Store

Download, install, and play directly on macOS.

🤖 ZayBot & Serendipity AI

Challenge the internal engine logic with specific personality biases ranging from completely passive to hyper-aggressive across 11 difficulty levels.

🤝 Local & Online Multiplayer

Play face-to-face on one computer with automatic board flipping, or connect across the internet via low-latency direct IP peer-to-peer matchmaking.

💾 Portable Sessions

Full serialization support. Save your games to `.chesslog` files, load them later, and use full undo/redo with AI-aware reconstruction.

Start Fast, Play Beautifully.

A completely redesigned home screen gets you into the action immediately. Skip the account creation and rating grinds—just jump straight into the game.

Drag and Drop Play

Move pieces seamlessly right from the home screen into matches. The intuitive drag-to-move interactions bring a tactile, premium feel to every game.

Rich Match Setup

Configuring a match versus the computer gives you total control. Play as white or black, and adjust the Serendipity Engine's aggressiveness before the clock starts.

Full Board Control

Supports both click-to-move and drag-and-drop mechanics. Visual highlights clearly indicate legal moves, the last move played, and check status, keeping your focus completely on the strategy.

Direct Multiplayer

Challenge your friends anywhere with direct IP multiplayer pairing. Host a game and wait for an opponent to join, leveraging low-latency peer-to-peer socket connections without any required servers or accounts.
