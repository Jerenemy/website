{% endcall %} {% call ui.sect("03", "The episode") %}

Sonar treats a podcast episode as first-class media: searchable, rateable, reviewable, and connected to the people whose taste you care about.

Each episode page collects its average rating, the distribution of ratings and the reviews of its listeners. Your own log becomes a searchable record of what you heard and how you felt about it.
