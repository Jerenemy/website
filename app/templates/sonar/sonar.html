Remember what you heard.

Sonar gives podcast listeners a durable log for ratings, reviews, favorites, and the episodes they want to keep on deck.

Built around the episode

Your listening history becomes useful.

Sonar treats podcast episodes like first-class media: searchable, rateable, reviewable, and connected to the people whose taste you care about.

  •    
    Personal log: Build a searchable record of what you listened to and how you felt about it.
  •    
    Community context: Episode pages collect average ratings, distributions, and listener reviews.
  •    
    Discovery: Search podcasts and episodes, browse trending shows, and keep a later list ready.
