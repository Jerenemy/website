FAQ.

Short answers for support questions that come up often.

Why is a podcast or episode missing?

Sonar searches the Podcast Index catalog. Some publishers have delayed metadata, alternate show names, or RSS feed changes. Send the title and RSS feed URL so it can be checked.

Why did my rating or review not appear immediately?

Sonar saves changes automatically and refreshes the feed after updates. If the feed looks stale, reopen the episode or profile page and check your network connection.

Can I delete or edit a review?

Reviews are tied to your episode log. Open the episode or review detail in Sonar to update the text, change the rating, or remove the log.
