MoinLLM

A Localized Large Language Model
Jeremy Zay

The Concept

Most Large Language Models are built to be generalists—they know a little bit about everything from everywhere. With MoinLLM, I wanted to explore the opposite approach. I set out to answer a highly specific research question: Can we build a localized "Hamburg AI"?

The goal was to fine-tune a model so that it didn't just possess facts about Hamburg, Germany, but actually understood the cultural, historical, and geographic nuances of the city like a true local.

The Process

To bring MoinLLM to life, I utilized cluster computing environments alongside Google's infrastructure. The model was trained using specific instructional data formatting, gradually teaching the AI to adopt a regional persona and prioritize localized data structures.

Discoveries & Results

The results of the fine-tuning process were highly encouraging. The training metrics showed strong learning progression, ultimately achieving a mean token accuracy of over 61%.

More importantly, the qualitative results showed that the model successfully adopted its intended identity. When prompted, it naturally identified itself as an AI specialized in the history and development of Hamburg.

When asked for directions from the Speicherstadt to the Reeperbahn, the model didn't just give a sterile route. It suggested driving along the Elbe riverbank to view the historic port facilities before diving into the multifaceted cultural landscape of the Reeperbahn.

This experiment proved that with the right fine-tuning architecture, AI models can move beyond generic, broad-stroke responses and provide deeply localized, context-aware interactions.
