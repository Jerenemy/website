Most large language models are generalists: they know a little about everything from everywhere. MoinLLM explores the opposite, around one research question: can we build a localized “Hamburg AI”?

The goal was to fine-tune a model so that it did not just hold facts about Hamburg, Germany, but understood the city’s cultural, historical and geographic nuances like a local.

{% endcall %} {% call ui.sect("02", "The process") %}

The model was trained on cluster computing environments alongside Google’s infrastructure, using instruction-formatted data that gradually taught it to adopt a regional persona and prioritize localized data.

LLM fine-tuning · Cluster computing · Natural language processing · Data formatting

{% endcall %} {% call ui.sect("03", "Results") %}

The training metrics showed steady learning progression, reaching a mean token accuracy of over 61%.

Qualitatively, the model adopted its intended identity: when prompted, it identified itself as an AI specialized in the history and development of Hamburg.

Asked for directions from the Speicherstadt to the Reeperbahn, the model did not give a sterile route. It suggested driving along the Elbe riverbank to see the historic port facilities before reaching the cultural landscape of the Reeperbahn.

With the right fine-tuning, a model can move beyond generic, broad-stroke answers to localized, context-aware ones.
