How do I make Markdown files open in Skylight?

Open Skylight's Settings and click Make Default. You can also do it in Finder: select a Markdown file, choose File › Get Info, pick Skylight under "Open with" and click Change All.

Quick Look doesn't use Skylight.

Open System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions, find Quick Look and turn on Skylight. If the preview still looks the same, open Skylight once, then try again.

Images next to my document don't show up.

Skylight can only read files you open. The first time a document points at images in its folder, Skylight asks for access to that folder. Allow it and Skylight will remember the folder for later.

Images from the web don't show up.

Check that "Load images from the web" is on in Skylight's Settings.

Can I edit files in Skylight?

No, Skylight only reads. Keep it open next to your editor: when you save, it reloads and stays at the same spot.
